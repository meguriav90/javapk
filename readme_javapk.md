# 🎬 JAVAPK - Free Japanese Movie Streaming App

**JAVAPK** is a lightweight, fast, and feature-rich Android streaming application built with Kotlin. It features a massive database containing **over 430,000 videos**, and with your continuous support, even more content and features will be added in future updates!

> ⚠️ **Disclaimer:** This application is intended for **adult audiences only (18+)**. By downloading and installing this app, you confirm that you meet the legal age requirement in your jurisdiction.

## ✨ Features

* 🔒 **Pattern Lock Protection:** Keep your app and viewing history private with built-in security.

* ⚡ **Lightweight & Fast:** Built natively with Kotlin for smooth performance and minimal battery/memory usage.

* 🔍 **Smart Search:** Easily find videos by title or search by your favorite actor/actress 🎭.

* 📱 **TikTok-Style View Interface:** Enjoy an alternative, modern vertical swipe interface for quick browsing.

* 🌃 **Built-in Streaming Viewer:** Smooth playback experience directly inside the app.

* ⏱️ **Custom Time Highlights:** Add custom timestamps/bookmarks to jump straight to your favorite moments.

* ⭐ **Favorites Management:** Save videos to your favorites list, with support to ❤️ **import and export** your data anytime.

* 🗄️ **Database Import:** Support for importing local databases (includes a base database of 50,000+ offline entries).

* 🔊 **Panic Close Shortcut:** Quickly close and exit the application instantly by pressing the **Volume Down** button.

## 📥 Download & Installation

1. **Download** the latest APK from our official website [javapk.com](https://javapk.com) or via the [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/endy23/alljavtv/releases) page.

2. Go to your Android **Settings > Security** and enable **"Install unknown apps"** for your browser or file manager.

3. Locate and open the downloaded `JAVAPK.apk` file.

4. Tap **Install** and launch the application.
